## Description (4000)
Relics: Consoles is a retro game player made for iPhone Duo's two screens from the ground up, and it plays just as well on
iPhone and iPad. It plays the classic game files you already own. Add your games from the
Files app and they appear on your shelf, ready to play. A few free homebrew games are already on the shelf, shared by
their authors under free licences, so you can try it straight away.

BUILD YOUR OWN CONTROLLER
Make the on-screen controller yours. Drag any button where your thumbs want it, make it bigger or smaller, fade it or
hide it. Every system, every game and every way you hold the phone keeps its own layout, so a racing game and a
platformer can each feel just right.

SYSTEMS
NES, SNES, Game Boy, Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance, Mega Drive, Nintendo DS and Nintendo 64.

ON IPHONE DUO
• Laptop pose: the game stands on the upright screen and a full-size controller lies flat under your thumbs. Three
  picture quick-save slots, rewind and fast-forward sit along the hinge.
• Two-screen games in laptop pose: the top screen stands up, the touch screen lies flat under your finger.
• Book pose: the game on the left page, the controls split across both pages. Two-screen games put one screen on each
  page.
• Close it to pause and save; open it to carry on exactly where you were.
• Nothing needs folding: flat, it's a portrait controller under the game, or turn it sideways for the controls beside
  the picture.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Save states with pictures, plus an automatic save when you leave a game
• Rewind a few seconds, or fast-forward at 2x to 8x
• Screen styles: sharp pixels, pixel-perfect scaling, scanlines and a handheld LCD look
• A six-button layout for the systems that use one
• Analog stick and camera buttons for three-dimensional games, with an optional camera stick
• Bluetooth game controllers and keyboards, with remapping, hotkeys and a second player
• Opens .zip files that hold one game

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no tracking and no internet connection. Your games and saves stay on your device, in the app's
folder in Files.
