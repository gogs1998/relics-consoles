paraLLEl-RDP, Granite (its Vulkan backend) and volk
====================================================
Used for: Nintendo 64 graphics (the RDP) rendered with Vulkan compute shaders on the GPU.
Source: parallel-rdp-standalone commit 388d70f (paraLLEl-RDP commit 1cecd04),
https://github.com/Themaister/parallel-rdp-standalone, vendored unmodified in
Packages/CoreKit/Sources/MUPEN64/parallel-rdp. volk (header version 269), https://github.com/zeux/volk,
is bundled in it. Vulkan-Headers, also bundled, are Apache-2.0 (see the MoltenVK file).
